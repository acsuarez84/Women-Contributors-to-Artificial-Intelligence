Understanding How LLMs Shape Latino Women's Voices

This educational platform explores the intersection of rhetoric, composition, and artificial intelligence. We examine how Large Language Models (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Co-Pilot) both enhance and minimize Latino women's rhetorical contributions, affecting culture, identity, and representation.
