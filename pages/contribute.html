Contribute to This Resource

Help improve this educational platform by submitting corrections, additions, and insights

Why Your Contributions Matter

This is a living educational resource. As an open platform examining how LLMs affect Latino women's rhetoric and representation, we depend on community input to:

  • Correct inaccuracies in historical information or analysis
  • Add missing contributors to the timeline
  • Share new research on LLMs and cultural representation
  • Provide lived experience that enriches understanding
  • Suggest improvements to accessibility and usability

Unique Feature: See How LLMs Respond

When you submit a correction, you can optionally see how LLMs might regenerate the content based on your input—and receive an explanation of the algorithmic implications of these changes. This demonstrates how LLMs handle correction and cultural context.

How to Contribute

See How LLMs Handle Your Corrections

After submitting a correction, you can optionally explore how different LLMs might regenerate the content. This feature requires your own API keys (stored locally).

Educational Purpose

This feature demonstrates:

  • How LLMs incorporate new information
  • Whether cultural context is preserved or lost in regeneration
  • How different models handle corrections differently
  • The algorithmic implications of using AI to update cultural content

This is for learning—not for automatically updating the site! All contributions are reviewed by humans before being incorporated.

Contribution Guidelines

What We're Looking For

  • Accurate information with verifiable sources
  • Respectful, constructive feedback
  • Diverse perspectives on rhetoric and LLMs
  • Missing voices in the historical timeline
  • Accessibility improvements

Timeline Contributions

When suggesting a timeline addition, please include:

  • Full name of the contributor
  • Birth/death years (or active years)
  • Field(s): rhetoric, composition, linguistics, AI, etc.
  • Key contributions: What did they contribute? Why is it significant?
  • Cultural/identity impact: How does this relate to Latino women's rhetoric?
  • Sources: Citations, URLs, or references

What We Can't Accept

  • Unverified or false information
  • Discriminatory or hateful content
  • Commercial promotions
  • Plagiarism
